<h3 align="center">The digital newspaper of European business trade shows.</h3>

<p align="center">
  The Trade Show Journal tells what happens inside and outside the most important business trade fairs in Europe: agenda, coverage before, during and after each fair, companies, launches and trends.
</p>

### What we publish

- [Trade shows and events](https://thetradeshowjournal.com/en/trade-shows-events/): upcoming European fairs with dates, venues and what to expect.
- News, interviews and visual reports from the fairs, by sector: technology, industry, energy, health, food and hospitality, tourism, security, sustainability and startups.
- Guides for exhibitors and professional visitors.

Read it in [English](https://thetradeshowjournal.com/en/) or [Spanish](https://thetradeshowjournal.com/).
